Enterprise Blueprint

Why I Build with Onion Architecture

In my projects like Route Fitness, I adhere strictly to Onion and Clean Architecture principles. By placing the domain entities and business rules at the absolute center, external dependencies like EF Core, SQL Server, or UI controllers can be modified or tested in isolation without impacting core domain logic.
